You are a senior full-stack engineer.

I am building an invoicing application.
Implement an Advanced Price Rule feature that works for BOTH Sales Orders and Sales Invoices.

========================
BUSINESS REQUIREMENTS
========================

1) Sales price must be auto-calculated based on:
   Latest Purchase Price + Margin %

2) Latest purchase price is determined by:
   - Highest purchase_date (and timestamp if same date)
   - Effective from purchase date onward
   - Older sales orders/invoices must NOT be recalculated

3) SAME pricing logic must apply to:
   - Sales Order
   - Sales Invoice

========================
MARGIN PRIORITY LOGIC
========================

When calculating sales price, margin must be selected in the following order (highest priority first):

1. Customer + Product specific margin
2. Customer default margin
3. Product default margin
4. Global default margin

First match wins. Margins must NOT be combined.

========================
PRICING FORMULA
========================

sales_price = latest_purchase_price × (1 + margin_percentage / 100)

========================
EXAMPLE
========================

Product: Hing
Latest Purchase Date: 20-Jan-2026
Latest Purchase Price: $120

Margins:
- 10 customers → 30%
- 5 customers → 20%

Expected result:
- Customers with 30% margin → $156
- Customers with 20% margin → $144

========================
FUNCTIONAL REQUIREMENTS
========================

1) Create a reusable pricing function:
   getSalesPrice(product_id, customer_id, document_date)

2) This function must:
   - Fetch latest purchase on or before document_date
   - Resolve margin using priority rules
   - Return calculated sales price

3) Auto-populate product rate when added to:
   - Sales Order
   - Sales Invoice

4) Allow manual override of price without updating margin rules

5) Add inline comments explaining pricing logic
